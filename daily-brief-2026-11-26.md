# Remote job brief — 2026-11-26

**Thin day: 2 roles worth your time, and only the first is a clean fit.** I screened about 270 postings from RemoteOK (API plus 7 tag feeds), Remotive (API), We Work Remotely (RSS), Jobspresso (feed), NoDesk (AI/Data/Internship pages) and Wellfound, and ran 7 web searches. Almost everything was US/EU-locked, senior, or older than 21 days.

### 1. Research Engineer, Privacy and Anonymization — Pragmatike (recruiting for a YC-backed AI training-data company)
- **Apply:** https://remoteOK.com/remote-jobs/remote-research-engineer-privacy-and-anonymization-pragmatike-1137792 (link checked: returns 200)
- **Source:** RemoteOK, posted 12 Nov 2026 (14 days ago)
- **Location terms:** The client has offices in San Francisco and Singapore. The posting says "remote candidates are welcome if their working hours can overlap about 70–80% with either San Francisco (US Pacific) or Singapore business hours." For you, Singapore hours (09:00–18:00 SGT) are **06:30–15:30 IST**, which you can cover. Avoid the PT option.
- **Why it fits:** The role builds PII/secret-detection pipelines that combine rules, classifiers and LLM methods, and benchmarks them on recall, precision and latency. That is the same kind of work as your TrueVision ensemble (10 models benchmarked, SMOTE for the rare fraud class) and your SQL Q&A Chain guardrails. The posting says "technical aptitude and learning potential matter more than years of experience."
- **Tailoring note:** Open with TrueVision as a rare-class detection problem: you benchmarked 10 models and used SMOTE to lift fraud recall. Then name your read-only SQL guardrail as privacy/safety-minded engineering. **Caveat:** the role is full-time and starts ASAP, so ask whether a part-time or intern arrangement is possible around your TIET schedule.

### 2. Software Engineer I – Backend — Abnormal AI (US email-security company)
- **Apply:** https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/abnormal-software-engineer-i-backend (link checked: returns 200; WWR asks you to create a free account to see the employer's apply link)
- **Source:** We Work Remotely, posted today
- **Location terms:** WWR tags it **"Asia Only."** The body says the role sits on the Critical Email Products team in **Singapore**. ⚠️ It does not say outright whether remote-from-India is allowed, so check this before you invest time. Singapore hours are 06:30–15:30 IST.
- **Why it fits:** It is entry-level (Engineer I) and asks for only 1+ years of backend experience. The stack is Python-first (Django, Spark, AWS) and the team works on AI-native email security. Your Node.js/Next.js portal backend and the Groq RAG pipeline you deployed are directly relevant.
- **Tailoring note:** Lead with the CoDSAI internship: an end-to-end deployed system with ~2-second RAG query latency. Expect the 1-year experience bar to be the main objection, and answer it with the HackSpire'25 win as evidence that you ship.

**Rejected leads worth knowing about:** Pinterest MLE II "Anywhere" (actually US-only), GitLab Backend Agentic (Poland geo-locked), Addepto Data Engineer (3+ years), Toptal Terminal-Bench (3+ years), Runmivo AI Agent Intern on Wellfound (reposted over a month ago and asks for 2 years), iMerit evaluator (expired). Askable AI Coding Tasks was already sent earlier.

**Today's read:** "Worldwide" labels on WWR are unreliable. Both Pinterest and GitLab turned out to be country-locked once opened, and the only real India-compatible openings were **Singapore-hours** roles. Searching for APAC/Singapore-overlap postings is worth more than searching "worldwide." A recurring gap: postings keep asking for **Docker, Kubernetes and AWS/GCP production experience**. One containerized deployment of your RAG project on a cloud VM would cover that on your CV.
